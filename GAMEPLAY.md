# Interface and Controls

### Dynamic Activation Key
With [Dynamic Activation Key](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/96273) (DAK), many actions have different, more intuitive behaviors when you press **Shift + Activate** (mouse & keyboard) or **X + Activate** (controller).

Some examples (this is not an exhaustive list):
- Taking or reading a book
- Taking or eating food or ingredients
- Taking or equipping gear
- Cooking, or bringing up the campsite perk trees when looking at the campsite fire
- Petting a dog or horse

Remember this exists, and try it on different interaction prompts.

---

### Feeding as a Werewolf
Use [Dynamic Activation Key (DAK)](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/96273) to feed on a target. Hold the hotkey (**Shift** on keyboard or **X** on controller) to activate feeding.

---

### Cycling Through Nearby Objects
With [Better Third Person Selection](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/64339), you can cycle through multiple activatable objects in front of you without repositioning.
- **Mouse & keyboard:** hold **LAlt** and use the **scroll wheel**.
- **Controller:** use **X + D-Pad Left/Right**.

---

### Dialogue Perspective Fix
While in dialogue, press **F** on keyboard or **RS** on controller to switch the point of view. This can be handy if you get odd or unfitting angles from the [Alternate Conversation Camera](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/21220) mod.

---

### Controller Map
If your controller setup isn't working properly when launching the game, open the **in-game Controls menu**, click **Reset to Defaults**, and make sure that:
- **Right Hand** is set to **RB**
- **Left Hand** is set to **LB**

If they aren't set correctly, assign them manually, then reload the game. This should resolve the issue.

### Bloodskal Blade
Due to how [BFCO](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/117052) works, the Bloodskal Blade's mechanics have been reworked with [Bloodskal Weapon Art](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/114317). Holding the left and right movement keys while performing normal attacks triggers **horizontal** waves, while holding them during power attacks triggers **vertical** waves.

# Survival

### Fast Travel
[Journeyman - A Fast Travel Overhaul](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/92220) disables fast travel unless you have a **travel pack** in your inventory.

- **Crafting:** 1 leather, 2 leather strips, 2 firewood, and 1 torch. Torches can be crafted with 1 firewood and 2 leather strips, or bought from merchants.
- **Buying:** travel packs are sold by general merchants (such as Belethor in Whiterun) and by innkeepers.

---

### Injuries
[Blade and Blunt - A Combat Overhaul](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/34549) adds an injury system. When your health drops below certain thresholds, incoming attacks have a chance to injure you. Blocking or casting a ward significantly reduces this chance.

Injuries progress through three stages: **minor**, **major** and **critical**. Each stage reduces your maximum health.

**Healing:** you must rest in a bed or bedroll.
- Shorter rests (4 or 8 hours) partially heal or downgrade injuries.
- A long rest (12 hours) fully cures all injuries.

If you don't rest and recover within 24 in-game hours, your injuries become **infected**, inflicting harsher debuffs that can worsen over time. Infections are treated as diseases and can be cured by any Cure Disease effect.

---

### Diseases
With [Cure Disease Overhaul](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/185399), cure disease potions no longer cure diseases instantly. They have become **Ease Disease Potions**: they lower the effectiveness of diseases by 50% for 10 minutes, and allow you to sleep for a minimum of 6 hours to remove all diseases.

---

### Gear Degradation
[Losing Your Temper - An Equipment Durability Mod](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/182493) degrades your gear, and NPC gear, when used in combat. To repair your gear, do one of the following:

- Go to any blacksmith and start a conversation. Choose the *Can you repair my gear for me?* dialogue option, then select the item you want repaired. This costs gold.
- Interact with a workbench (for damaged armor) or a grindstone (for damaged weapons), then select the damaged item. This costs the materials listed in the crafting screen.

---

### Washing
With [Bathing in Skyrim](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/135288), you accumulate dirt as time passes. To wash yourself, use soap or washing rags from your inventory. You can buy them from merchants or find them while looting.

---

### Stress and Fear
With [Stress and Fear - A Dynamic Sanity System](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/116522), getting hurt in combat causes stress. Stress can be reduced by a variety of activities, such as drinking, having a warm meal, taking a bath, petting an animal, playing music, praying, relaxing at an inn, etc.

# Quests

### Starting Specific Quests
Most quests start the vanilla way, with a few exceptions for roleplay and progression balance. Here is a breakdown of the changes:

- **Main Quest:** with [Skyrim Unbound](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/27962), the first dragons appear at word walls once you reach level 15, and in the wilderness at level 25. Once they do, slay one and the Greybeards will summon you, starting the main quest. This timing is configurable in the mod's MCM.
- **Dawnguard DLC:** with [Sensible Dawnguard Start](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/162621), instead of simply reaching level 10, you must also complete [Laid to Rest](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Laid_to_Rest) to begin the Dawnguard questline.
- **The Thieves Guild:** with [Thieves Guild Requirements SE](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/33256), you must meet the following requirements before starting the recruitment quest: a minimum Sneak skill of 25, at least 5 pockets picked, and 25 items stolen. In short, you need to be a thief.
- **The Forsworn Conspiracy:** with [Delayed Quest Starts](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/72751), [The Forsworn Conspiracy](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:The_Forsworn_Conspiracy) doesn't start until you reach level 25. Once you do, talk to Kibell, the carriage driver of Markarth, and ask him "What do you know about Markarth?" followed by "Bent a few folks' arms the wrong way?"
- **Creation Club content:** with [Rebalancing Anniversary Edition - Quest Requirements](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/61004), Creation Club quests now have level requirements. You can check these requirements (and adjust them to your preference) in the mod's MCM.
- **Divine Crusader:** see the [UESP page](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Divine_Crusader) and the [Quest Guide](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/articles/3005).
- **VIGILANT:** to start [VIGILANT SE](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/11849), you need to be level 25 and have completed [The House of Horrors](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:The_House_of_Horrors). Then head to the Windpeak Inn in Dawnstar.
- **Acquiring Myrwatch:** you need at least 350 magicka for your spell to affect the seal and gain access to Myrwatch.
